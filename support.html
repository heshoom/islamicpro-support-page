Quick troubleshooting

  • Ensure Location Services are enabled for the app.
  • Check your device clock/timezone are set correctly.
  • Restart the app after changing settings.
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store.

Common questions

Why are prayer times different?

Prayer times depend on selected calculation method and juristic settings. Verify your method in Settings  Prayer Times.
